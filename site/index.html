kauk

one interface to bind them all — a control-plane package platform.

KAUK is a native index for first-class artifacts — packages, skills, and arbitrary files — plus a federation layer that publishes to and pulls from npm, PyPI, OCI and GitHub when you opt in. One CLI, one grammar, one auth flow over many backends. Your credentials stay on your machine.

Status: early. The kauk name is reserved on PyPI and npm; the CLI ships at 0.1.0.

Packages are served under kauk.ai/<pkg>, generated at publish.
