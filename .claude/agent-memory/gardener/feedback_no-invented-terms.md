Speak in the system's own vocabulary: badge fields (todo/functional/done,
queued/working/blocked-on-answers/plan-ready/complete), doctrine terms in
their defined meaning, plain English otherwise. Never coin shorthand, and
never stretch an existing term beyond its defined scope (e.g. "parked" is for
board tasks between builds — not MOOD pending reminders).

**Why:** Operator, 2026-08-18 — "I'm forbidding you to invent your own terms"
after "parked" was applied to MOOD pending items; undefined words force him to
ask what they mean.

**How to apply:** Before using a status-like word, check it's a badge value or
a doctrine term used in its defined sense; otherwise say it in plain words
("waiting on its trigger", "not started"). Related:
[[task-references-use-titles]], [[suggestions-rank-last]].
