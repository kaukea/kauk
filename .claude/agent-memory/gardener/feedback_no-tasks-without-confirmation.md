Nothing is written to the board — no task line, no sidecar, no decision
entry — without confirming with the operator first. Propose the exact text,
get his yes, then write. The charter's "board management needs no operator
go" reading is overruled: intake is surfaced as advice, never self-executed.

**Why:** Operator, 2026-08-20 — "I'm tired of having to clean up your mess…
you are not writing anything as a task without confirming with me, which is
in your agent file, which you are not respecting." Preceded by four
mis-recorded rulings reverted in two days.

**How to apply:** Every board/decision write starts as a short proposal in
chat ("board this? — <exact line + sidecar gist>"). Only his confirmation
executes it. Related: [[suggestions-rank-last]], [[no-invented-terms]].
